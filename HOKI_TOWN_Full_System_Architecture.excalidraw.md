# 🎨 HOKI TOWN — Complete 6-Tier Architecture Topology

ผังแสดงสถาปัตยกรรมทั้ง 6 ลำดับชั้นของเกม **HOKI TOWN** ตั้งแต่การรับอินพุตผู้เล่นบนมือถือ/พีซี ไปจนถึงการจัดเก็บข้อมูลถาวรบนคลาวด์

## 🧩 6-Tier Architecture Summary
1. **Tier 1: Client Devices (PC & Mobile Hybrid):** ContextActionService Sink, Spacebar PBrake Override, Tap-to-Drift Dual Pedal, Dynamic Touch Thumbstick, Raycast Smoke Snap.
2. **Tier 2: Local Simulation & Prediction:** CDT Arcade Drive Engine, 18,000 N Rear Drift Lock, 1.2s Safe Traction Restore, 60 FPS Gauge Rendering.
3. **Tier 3: Network Transport (ReplicatedStorage.Remotes):** `VehicleRepairRequest`, `VehicleImpactEvent`, `HarvestCropRequest`, `FetchPlayerData`, Rate Limiting & Anti-Cheat Distance Clamp.
4. **Tier 4: Server Authoritative Backend (ServerScriptService):** VehicleService Spawner, Centralized Collision Tiers, GodMode Gate, Fuel Drain Loop, HazardService Cascade, Farming Validator.
5. **Tier 5: World DataModel & Streaming:** TeslaRoadster Chassis, 10 Explosive Barrels, Kinematic Totems, 1,552 Modular Roads, Shophouses & FiveM Warehouses.
6. **Tier 6: Cloud Persistence (ProfileStore):** Session-Locked Profile Data Store, Cash & Bank, Garage Array, Inventory Stash, Auto-Save every 5 min.
